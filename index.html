How everything works

The same six surfaces on every platform — only the navigation shape changes (bottom dock on phones, a left sidebar on desktop).

Home

Tap a task to open its detail. Swipe right to mark it done, left to move it to Trash — both show an Undo toast for a few seconds. The round + adds a task; the suitcase icon next to it opens the dedicated trip planner. Category chips filter the list, and the dashed "Custom" chip lets you add, rename, or remove your own categories. Day / Week / Month switches the checklist's time scope, and the sort pill cycles between due date, priority, and category.

Calendar

Tap any day to see or add tasks for it. Tap the month name at the top to open a year → month → day picker spanning a century in either direction. On a mouse or trackpad, drag a task from the day list onto a different day in the grid to reschedule it. "Export month (.ics)" downloads a standard calendar file your phone's Calendar app can import.

Trip planner

Set a name, destination, and a single departs/returns date field for each end — tap either date to open the same month picker. The packing checklist arrives pre-loaded and fully editable. Toggle "Add items to your normal task list" to also surface any packing item as its own task on Home, with an optional note.

Filters

Combine status, category, priority, timing, and an optional custom date range — each with a short explanation right under its label. Apply sends the filtered result straight to Home, with a "Custom filter · Clear" pill to back out.

Insights

A completion ring, a day-by-day bar chart, and a category breakdown for the current week, plus your current and best streaks. Every figure is a real count from your own tasks — nothing here is a forecast or estimate.

Profile

Set your name on first launch (required to enter the app), optionally connect an email, and switch between Dark and Light themes. Notifications, Trash, and Help & support — including replaying this tutorial — all live here too.

A note on reminders

Browser reminders (Profile → Notifications) fire while the app is open or recently active. On iPhone/iPad specifically, Safari can't send notifications from a regular browser tab at all — that's an iOS restriction, not a bug. Add TaskDue to your Home Screen first (see the install guide above), and reminders work from the installed app.

Vibration isn't supported by iOS Safari under any circumstances. Everywhere else — Android, Mac, Windows — reminders and vibration work as expected once installed.
